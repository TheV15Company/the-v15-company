Payment Terms

Clear & Transparent Terms

We believe transparency should be part of every creator-brand collaboration.

For Creators
✦ 10% agency fee from the creator's final collaboration amount.
✦ Creator payment cycle targeted within 15 days from receiving brand payment.
✦ Payment terms are communicated before the campaign begins.
For Brands
✦ 10% agency fee from the final campaign amount.
✦ Brand payment cycle targeted within 5 days as agreed.
✦ Advance payment may be requested for campaign security.

Creator Safety Comes First

✓ Never share your OTP, UPI PIN or banking password for a collaboration.
✓ Always verify suspicious payment requests and links.
✓ Campaign requirements and payment terms should be clear before starting.
✓ If something feels suspicious, contact our team before proceeding.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How can creators join The V15 Company? +
Creators can join our network by submitting their details through our creator registration form. Our team can then consider them for relevant opportunities.
Do creators have to pay to join? +
Joining the creator network does not require an upfront registration fee. Agency fees, if applicable to a collaboration, are communicated clearly before the campaign.
What niches do you work with? +
We work with creators across multiple categories including technology, lifestyle, fashion, beauty, travel, food, gaming, finance, education and UGC.
How do brands start a campaign? +
Brands can contact our team with their campaign requirements, budget, target audience, deliverables and preferred creator categories.
Are payment terms communicated before collaboration? +
Yes. Campaign deliverables, payment terms and other important requirements should be communicated before the collaboration begins.
